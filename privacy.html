Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Who we are

ProFinder is operated by KASITU Webs ("we"). Contact: info@kasituwebs.co.za.

What we collect

Service providers: full name, business name, cell number, email, area, service description, business or trade registration number, two references (names and numbers), an uploaded ID or proof document, and your account login.
Customers: you can search without an account. If you report a listing we store your report text. If you write a review we store your email address (never shown), the name you choose to display, your rating and comment.

Why we collect it

To verify providers before they are listed, to show approved listings to customers, to contact you about your application, and to handle reports and keep the platform safe.

What is public

Only after approval: your business name, name, area, categories, description and cell number. Your ID, registration number, references and email are private and visible only to you and our administrators.

Who processes data for us

Supabase (database, logins and file storage, hosted in Ireland), Render (website hosting), Brevo (sending emails) and Cloudflare (bot protection). Your data may therefore be processed outside South Africa.

References

If you give us a reference, you confirm that person agreed to be contacted. We use their details only to verify your application.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. Rejected or withdrawn applications and their documents are deleted within [90] days. You may ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask to see, correct or delete your information, and object to its use. Email info@kasituwebs.co.za. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Security

Documents are stored privately, access is restricted to administrators, and passwords are handled by our login provider. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected people if a breach affects them.
